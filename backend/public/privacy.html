Privacy Policy & Data Governance

1. Introduction & Overview

Signature Detailing ("we," "our," or "us"), operating the automotive styling, Paint Protection Film (PPF), Ceramic Coating, and detailing studio located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India, is committed to safeguarding your privacy and personal data.

This Privacy Policy describes how our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, automated consultation messaging assistants, and web applications collect, process, and protect your information when you interact with us via Instagram Direct Messages (DMs), Instagram Comments, WhatsApp, or our official studio web applications.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily choose to provide when inquiring about vehicle protection packages, booking detailing bay slots, or requesting customer support:

  • Direct Message & Comment Content: Questions, inquiries, and messages sent via Instagram Direct Messages, Instagram Comments, or WhatsApp.
  • Instagram Public Profile Details: Your Instagram username, display name, and unique numeric Instagram-Scoped User ID (IGSID) assigned by Meta.
  • Customer Detailing Specs: Your full name, mobile number, car make and model (e.g., Porsche 911, Audi A6, Thar), requested package (Paint Protection Film, 10H Ceramic Coating, 💎 Graphene Coating, Paint Correction, Interior Detailing), and appointment preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information exclusively for legitimate automotive detailing business operations:

  • To provide instant, accurate package recommendations, pricing estimates, and warranty breakdowns.
  • To schedule vehicle physical inspections and cleanroom bay slots at our Bhubaneswar studio.
  • To notify our master detailers and studio director of your inquiry so we can follow up promptly on WhatsApp or phone.
  • To maintain client service records, warranty certificates, and vehicle maintenance timelines.
🔒 Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal contact information to any third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing networks. Your data remains strictly within Signature Detailing.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

Your data is hosted in tier-1 cloud infrastructure powered by Supabase and Vercel. We enforce strict technical and organizational safeguards:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All communication between Meta Graph API, our CRM webhooks, and your browser is encrypted via modern TLS 1.3 / SSL.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Studio dashboard access is protected behind studio passcodes and encrypted administrative session controls.
  • Automated Data Retention & Pruning: Inquiries are retained only as long as necessary to service your vehicle and honor warranties, after which they are archived or permanently purged.

5. Compliance with Meta Platform Terms

Our integration strictly complies with Meta's Developer Terms, Instagram Messaging API Policies, and WhatsApp Business Policies:

  • We only interact with users who initiate contact (inbound DM, post/reel comment, or website consultation form).
  • All tokens and Meta Graph API credentials are stored securely in encrypted server environment variables and never exposed to client-side code.
  • We adhere to Meta's data minimization principles and only request the essential permissions required for client service: instagram_basic, instagram_manage_messages, and instagram_manage_comments.

6. User Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with Meta Platform Policies, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, and global privacy standards (GDPR/CCPA), you have the right to request the complete deletion of your records at any time.

Option 1: Email Deletion Request (Processed within 24 Hours)

Send an email with the subject "Data Deletion Request" to:

📧 Email: signaturedetailing.bbsr@gmail.com

Please mention your Instagram handle (e.g., @yourusername) or mobile number. Our data officer will purge all conversations, contact info, and lead records from our database within 24 business hours.

Option 2: Revoke Meta App Access via Instagram App

You can revoke app permissions directly from your Instagram mobile app at any time:

  • Open Instagram → Go to Settings and privacy.
  • Tap Website permissions (or Apps and websites).
  • Select Signature CRM and tap Remove / Revoke Access.
