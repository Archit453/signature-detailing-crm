1. Overview of Data Deletion Policy

In strict compliance with Meta Developer Platform Policies, GDPR, and Indian DPDP regulations, Signature Detailing guarantees that every user has the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of their personal information and message history collected by our Instagram and WhatsApp integrations.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your records via either of the following two options:

1 Option A: Send an Email Deletion Request (Recommended)

Send an email to our official privacy desk with the subject "Data Deletion Request":

📧 Email: signaturedetailing.bbsr@gmail.com

Please provide at least one of the following identifiers so we can locate your record:

  • Your Instagram Username (e.g., @yourusername)
  • Your Contact Phone Number (e.g., +91 9876543210)

2 Option B: Revoke App Permissions via Instagram

You can instantly disconnect your Instagram account from our CRM at any time from within your Instagram mobile app:

  1. Open the Instagram app on your mobile device.
  2. Go to your Profile → tap the menu icon (three lines) → Settings and privacy.
  3. Scroll down and tap Website permissions (or Apps and websites).
  4. Tap Active → locate Signature CRM.
  5. Tap Remove / Revoke Access.

3. What Happens When You Request Deletion?

Upon receipt of your request, our system administrator takes the following actions within 24 business hours:

  • Conversations & Chat History: All incoming and outgoing message logs associated with your Instagram IGSID or WhatsApp phone number are permanently erased from our Supabase database.
  • Lead Record & Customer Profiles: Any contact entries, vehicle notes, and inquiry records in our CRM pipeline are hard-deleted.
  • Confirmation Notice: An email confirmation containing your deletion confirmation ticket is dispatched to verify that no residual data remains.
